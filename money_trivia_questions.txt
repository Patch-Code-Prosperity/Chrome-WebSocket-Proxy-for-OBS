Basic Trivia Questions:
What year was the modern $2 bill introduced?
Answer: 1976

What coin features Franklin D. Roosevelt on the front?
Answer: Dime

Which U.S. Mint uses the "P" mint mark?
Answer: Philadelphia

Which U.S. coin has a shield on the reverse, introduced in 2010?
Answer: Penny

What is the smallest denomination of U.S. paper currency currently in circulation?
Answer: $1

Which president is featured on the front of the modern $2 bill?
Answer: Jefferson

What animal is depicted on the reverse of the Jefferson Nickel from 1938 to 2003?
Answer: Monticello (trick question, it’s not an animal)

Which U.S. Mint facility is located in Colorado?
Answer: Denver

What coin replaced the Buffalo Nickel in 1938?
Answer: Jefferson Nickel

What is the term for paper currency printed by the Bureau of Engraving and Printing?
Answer: Notes

Which coin design was replaced by the Lincoln Memorial design in 1959?
Answer: Wheat

What mint mark was added to a penny in 2019 as a first-time feature?
Answer: W (West Point)

Which $2 bill series features a red seal?
Answer: 1928

Which U.S. banknotes larger than $100 are still legal tender but no longer printed?
Answer: $500 and $1,000

What is the nickname of the $20 bill due to its association with counterfeit prevention?
Answer: The “Counterfeit Catcher”

Which $1 coin was minted in the 1970s featuring a famous U.S. president?
Answer: Eisenhower Dollar

What year was the first Federal Reserve Note issued in the U.S.?
Answer: 1914

What feature distinguishes the 1928 $2 bill from the modern version?
Answer: Red seal

What type of coin features Sacagawea starting in 2000?
Answer: Dollar coin

What error on the 1942-D over 1941 Mercury Dime makes it highly sought after?
Answer: Overdate

What is the highest denomination U.S. Federal Reserve Note ever printed?
Answer: $10,000

Answer: $10,000
